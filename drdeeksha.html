ABOUT DR. DEEKSHA

Meet Dr. Deeksha Gupta

Dr. Deeksha Gupta is a highly qualified Pediatrician and Neonatologist committed to delivering exceptional healthcare for newborns, infants, children and adolescents.

Having completed MBBS from Maulana Azad Medical College as a Gold Medalist, followed by MD Pediatrics and DM Neonatology from AIIMS, she brings outstanding academic excellence and clinical expertise to Healix Hospital.

She specializes in Level III NICU care, premature baby management, immunization, newborn care, pediatric emergencies, growth monitoring and developmental assessment while ensuring every family receives personalized support throughout their healthcare journey.
